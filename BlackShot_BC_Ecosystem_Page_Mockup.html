STEP 01 · EARN

How to Earn BC

There are multiple ways to earn BC, and the simplest one is just to play. Jump into a match and you're already earning.

In-match · BC pickup

Earn BC by playing matches

You earn BC based on your playtime during matches. The more you play, the more you earn.

1 BC is collected every 4 minutes of gameplay. BC is not awarded if you abandon the match.

Results screen · earnings

Track earnings on the results screen

Check exactly how much BC you earned at the end of every match on the results screen.

When a BC Boost event is active, you'll see BC raining across the screen.

Got too many unused weapons collecting dust? Break them down and turn them into BC.

Loadout · Dismantle

Dismantle weapons into BC

Dismantling is done in your Loadout, and only unused weapons purchased with Gems can be dismantled.

BC gained depends on the tier, duration, and type of each weapon.

Dismantle · BC result

See the BC you earned from dismantling

This window displays the BC obtained from dismantling your weapons.

Dismantling cannot be reversed.

STEP 02 · USE

How to Use BC

Your earned BC can be spent in the BC Shop and on our Mega Jackpot Games.

BC Shop

Spend your BC in the BC Shop

Head into battle and gear up at the BC Shop. Every item is within your reach, earned through your own play and dedication.

Some items have a limited quantity per server, and some have a purchase limit per character.

Mega Jackpot Games

Play the Mega Jackpot Games

Feeling lucky? Wager your BC on our Mega Jackpot Games for a chance to multiply your balance, right here on the webpage.
